# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant v2.1.

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

- Be respectful and constructive in feedback.
- Assume good intent; disagree on ideas, not people.
- Do not publish trace data, screenshots or logs containing other people's secrets or PII.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers via GitHub. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation in response to violations.

Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
